About These Places

From white sandbars and turquoise waterfalls to misty mountains and hidden caves, these five spots show how varied the Philippines can be. Here is a closer look at each one.

White Island, Camiguin

A crescent-shaped sandbar of fine white sand floating off the coast of Camiguin. Clear turquoise water surrounds it, while the green, cloud-capped mountains of the island rise in the distance.

Sumaguing Cave, Sagada

Also known as the Big Cave, Sumaguing is filled with warm, golden-orange limestone formations that hang like flowing curtains. Shallow pools of cool water gather on the cave floor.

Kawasan Falls, Cebu

Found in Badian, Cebu, Kawasan Falls spills down tiers of rock into a bright turquoise pool, framed by thick, lush green rainforest.

Mount Pulag, Benguet

Seen through pine branches, the Cordillera mountains of Benguet unfold in green ridges, terraced slopes and deep valleys under a blue sky full of white clouds.

Kayangan Lake, Coron

In Coron, Palawan, steep limestone cliffs covered in greenery surround the clear blue-green water of Kayangan Lake. A wooden walkway and boats line the shore.

Chocolate Hills, Bohol

In Carmen, Bohol, hundreds of cone-shaped, grass-covered hills roll across the land. In the dry season the grass turns brown, which gives the hills their name.
